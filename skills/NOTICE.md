# Development method skill credits

The nine development methods in this directory adapt the methods of
[oh-my-hermes](https://github.com/rlaope/oh-my-hermes), revision
`59270ab48e82ab24cfe4f445dc5693b97d4141a3` (MIT), from a supplied standalone
adaptation. Sources: verification-gate, app-debugging, build-failure-triage,
perf, qa, agent-debug, agent-evaluation, issue-requirements (upstream
ulw-interview), and failure-signal-audit.

LINA shortens the methods, removes environment-specific tooling and procedures,
and leaves routing, checks, authority and lifecycle enforcement in Core.
LINA's adaptations are distributed under Apache-2.0 with this MIT notice retained.
No upstream runtime or other skills are included.
